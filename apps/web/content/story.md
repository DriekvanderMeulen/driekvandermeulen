### High school

High school and I never fully aligned. Traditional studying and structured homework were difficult for me, which eventually meant moving from VWO to VMBO TL. Later in life I learned why, and more importantly, how to work with the way I focus. What stayed with me is that progress comes from taking responsibility for your own development. Step by step I learned how to structure my work, manage my attention and turn challenges into something productive.

#

### Global Exchange

After that period I joined Xplore for a three week global citizen exchange in India. It was the first time I stepped far outside my own environment. We supported local communities by helping build school facilities and teaching English to children. The experience was intense and inspiring because it taught me how much perspective you gain when you choose to operate outside your comfort zone. It strengthened my interest in people, culture and purpose driven work.

#

### Volunteer work

When I returned home I wanted to contribute closer to where I lived. For four years I volunteered at my local scouting group and guided children aged one to eleven each week. It required patience, clear communication and consistency. More than anything it taught me how to show up for others and stay reliable even when life gets busy. These lessons shaped how I approach teamwork today.

#

### Gaming

In my free time I enjoy strategy games. They let me experiment, think several steps ahead and work with complex systems. These games challenge the same problem solving mindset I use in my work because they combine creativity, structure and long term thinking. My current favourite is EU5 which is a surprisingly good way to keep my brain sharp.

#

### Music

During the lockdowns I started experimenting with electronic music. Mixing and producing gave me a creative outlet that felt very different from anything I had done before. Music keeps me curious and open to new ideas because it rewards experimentation and play. I try to bring that same curiosity and willingness to explore into my projects.

#

### Travel

Travel is the one thing I consistently invest in because it broadens my perspective. My first trip to Japan confirmed that. The culture, the food and the intentional way people move through daily life resonated with me. It reminded me that there are many ways to approach life and creativity. I plan to return in 2027 to explore the southern regions and continue learning from the country that inspires me most.
