## Soft skills

### Problem solving

I take messy problems and shape them into something clear and workable. I rely on logic, research and experimentation to find a solid path forward.

#

### Human centered design

I consider the person behind the product and adjust the design to support them without adding friction.

#

### Adaptability and continuous growth

I adjust quickly when the situation changes and I enjoy learning. I ask questions, try new approaches and improve my work through feedback.

#

### Teamwork

I work well with others because I like exchanging ideas, discussing different viewpoints and building better solutions together.

#

### Communication and empathy

I communicate in a way that stays clear and easy to follow. I listen well, ask the right questions and try to understand what others need.
