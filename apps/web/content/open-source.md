## Fig Grids

github: https://github.com/driekvandermeulen/fig-grid

When I build a website from a figma design, I often struggle with precisely matching the design. This is where Fig Grid comes in. It's a simple tool that helps you create grids that match those created in figma designs exactly as they are.

## Dev Sweep

github: https://github.com/driekvandermeulen/dev-sweep

Storage space is limited on your laptop or computer and cleaning up your system is a pain. Dev Sweep is a simple tool that helps you clean up your development environment by removing unused files and folders.
